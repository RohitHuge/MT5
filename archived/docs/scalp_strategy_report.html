1. How the Strategy Works

This is a trend-following momentum scalper. It watches three moving averages on the 5-minute XAUUSD chart and only takes trades when price momentum is already committed in one direction. It never tries to predict — it only joins a move that has already started.

Three tools work together on every candle:

IndicatorWhat it doesRole in the strategy
EMA 9 (fast line)Reacts quickly to recent price movementShort-term momentum signal
EMA 21 (slow line)Reacts slower — smooths out noiseMedium-term direction baseline
EMA 50 (trend line)Shows the prevailing trendTrend filter — no counter-trend trades
RSI 14 (momentum)Measures whether price is gaining or losing energyRejects weak/uncertain crossovers

The Core Signal: EMA Crossover

When the fast line (EMA9) crosses above the slow line (EMA21), it means short-term momentum has just turned bullish. When it crosses below, momentum has turned bearish. This is the crossover signal — it fires every time the lines swap positions.

▲ BUY — All 3 conditions required

  1. EMA9 crosses above EMA21 (this candle)
  2. Candle close is above EMA50 (uptrend confirmed)
  3. RSI14 is above 55 (upward momentum confirmed)

▼ SELL — All 3 conditions required

  1. EMA9 crosses below EMA21 (this candle)
  2. Candle close is below EMA50 (downtrend confirmed)
  3. RSI14 is below 45 (downward momentum confirmed)

Stop-Loss Placement

The stop-loss is placed at the lowest point of the last 4 candles (for BUY) or the highest point of the last 4 candles (for SELL), plus a 15-point buffer. This anchors the stop at a real price level where the market already showed support/resistance — not at an arbitrary distance from entry.

Take-Profit Placement

Take-profit is set at 1.5× the stop-loss distance from the actual fill price. So if the SL is 100 points from entry, the TP is 150 points from entry. This 1.5:1 risk-reward means the bot only needs to win 40% of trades to be profitable — a low bar that leaves room for error.

2. Risk Management Rules

RuleSettingWhat it means in practice
Per-trade risk 0.5% of equity On a $10,000 account: max $50 risk per trade. If equity grows to $11,000, risk per trade becomes $55.
Daily profit target $100 / day Once the account is up $100 in a day, no new trades open. The profit is locked in and the bot sleeps.
Daily loss limit $200 / day If the account drops $200 in a day, no new trades open. Prevents a bad day from becoming a disaster.
Max concurrent positions 2 trades The bot can hold 2 trades open at the same time (e.g. one BUY from London open, one SELL later).
Sessions traded London + New York Entries only allowed during London (08:00–17:00 BST) and New York (08:00–17:00 EST). Tokyo and dead hours are blocked.
Why these numbers? The daily target ($100) is 2 winning trades worth of profit. The daily loss ($200) is 4 losing trades. So on the worst possible day — 4 consecutive losses — the bot stops. That's a 2% drawdown on a $10K account for one bad day. Mathematically, at a 45% win rate you'd hit the daily loss limit roughly once per 15 trading days on average.

4. Why the RSI Filter Makes a Difference

An EMA crossover happens every time two lines swap position — but not every crossover is meaningful. The RSI filter rejects crossovers where the underlying momentum is weak or confused.

ScenarioEMA crossesRSI at crossingSignal taken?What typically happens
Price trending up strongly EMA9 above EMA21 ✓ 65 (above 55) ✓ YES Trade has momentum behind it, higher chance of hitting TP
Price drifting sideways, random cross EMA9 above EMA21 ✓ 51 (below 55) ✗ REJECTED Price likely reverses shortly after entry — these were the main losers
Price bouncing in choppy range EMA9 above EMA21 ✓ 48 (below 55) ✗ REJECTED Whipsaw trade — hits SL within a few candles
Strong downtrend resuming EMA9 below EMA21 ✓ 38 (below 45) ✓ YES Sell trade with momentum — good chance of reaching TP

In numbers: the RSI filter removed 194 raw signals from the 674 total (28% filtered out). Those removed signals had a low win rate. Keeping only the high-confidence signals raised the overall win rate from 42.4% → 45.1% and cut drawdown from 7.7% → 4.6%.
