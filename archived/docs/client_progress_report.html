Section 5

Strategy Explanations

Simple visual explanations of the two strategies currently running and four new strategies proposed for the daily bot. All examples use XAUUSD (Gold) prices.

Current Strategy: Liquidity Sweep

📖
How Liquidity Sweep Works in Plain English: Big traders (banks) park large buy orders just below obvious price levels. When price briefly dips below that level, it triggers retail stop-losses and fills the big buyers' orders. Price then snaps back up sharply. We enter on the snap-back. This gives us a high-quality, low-risk entry.

Limitation: Only 0–2 setups per day. Good for swing trades, not suitable for a "make money every single day" daily bot.

New Strategies for the Daily Bot

Four strategies suited for high-frequency M1/M5 trading with a daily profit target. These generate 5–25 signals per session — enough to hit a daily goal of $20–$50.

📈
EMA Ribbon Scalping
M1 chart · 15–25 trades/day

Three EMAs (3, 8, 21) must all align in the same direction before entering. When EMA3 > EMA8 > EMA21 and all sloping up → BUY. Exit when fast EMA flips.

Example: Gold is at $2,050. EMA3=2051, EMA8=2049, EMA21=2046 (all pointing up). Enter BUY at $2,050, TP=$2,060, SL=$2,045. Closes in ~3 minutes.

~55% Win Rate 1:1.2 R:R London + NY
〰️
Bollinger Band Bounce
M5 chart · 8–15 trades/day

Price tends to return to its average. When Gold touches the lower Bollinger Band, buy expecting a move back to the middle band. When it touches the upper, sell.

Example: Gold at $2,030, lower band at $2,030, middle band at $2,048. BUY at $2,030, TP=$2,048 (+18 pts), SL=$2,023 (-7 pts). Holds ~15 minutes.

~65% Win Rate 1:2.5 R:R Best in ranging markets
🔔
London Open Breakout
M5 chart · 1–2 trades/day

Between 7:00–8:00 UTC, Gold consolidates as traders wait for London to open. At 8:00 UTC the first big candle direction sets the tone for the morning session.

Example: 7–8 UTC range is $2,040–$2,055. At 8:00 price breaks above $2,055 → BUY. TP = $2,070, SL = $2,047. Usually resolves within 30–60 minutes.

~60% Win Rate 1:2.0 R:R 8:00–10:00 UTC only
🔀
RSI Divergence Scalp
M1 chart · 5–10 trades/day

Price makes lower lows but RSI makes higher lows — that disagreement signals a reversal. High-confidence entries because the momentum is visibly changing.

Example: Gold drops from $2,060 → $2,050 → $2,044 (lower lows). RSI at same points: 25 → 28 → 32 (higher lows). BUY at $2,044, TP=$2,058, SL=$2,040.

~62% Win Rate 1:2.0 R:R All active sessions
